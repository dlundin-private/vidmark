## The region

### Järnfors, the region of the made

**Design:** the builders' city at the great dam. Halvar, the master builder, refused to offer to Maren. When the dam started to fail she hollowed him in front of his apprentices and held it with his memories. His echo later raised the cities she is remembered for. His widow, Brita, still feeds a man who no longer knows her.

**Draft:** a steep river city of locks, forges and stone stairs in a gorge, below a dam that holds back a long mountain lake with drowned villages under it. The dam is called Maren's Dam, and her statue stands on its crest. It is failing again for two reasons:

1. **The Tithe.** As in Kallsjö, Kronvall took too much. The dam-wrights have forgotten how to read the stone, where the drains run and why the old relief cut was sealed.
2. **The flaw.** Hollowed memories bring the victim's habits and flaws (**Design**). **Draft:** Halvar refused because he had told the council the dam they wanted was too high. Maren took his skill, not his caution, and built it anyway. A century of ice has found the flaw.

### Whose domain the Keystone holds

**Design:** regions and Keystones are fixed; each Keystone's domain adapts to what the player lacks.

**Draft rule:** Järnfors' Keystone holds Craft and Runhem's holds Thought; a Keystone that would duplicate the start holds Kin instead.

| Start | Järnfors Keystone | Runhem Keystone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kin | Craft | Thought |
| Craft (the slice) | **Kin** | Thought |
| Thought | Craft | Kin |

With a Craft start, Signe already holds the region's own domain. Craft can mend the dam, but not in time. What she lacks is Kin.

### The region's question

**Draft:** *What would you take to save people you don't know?*

Chapter 1 asks what you would give for someone you love. Chapter 2 widens it to strangers and turns *give* into *take*. Maren's answer was one man for a city.

## The Keystone: Edda

**Draft throughout.**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Who** | Edda, about forty, Halvar and Brita's granddaughter: Brita was carrying their child when he was hollowed. Edda runs the Hollow-house, where the city tends the people the Tithe has emptied. |
| **Domain** | Kin. Her deepest memories are of caring for bodies that can't thank her: her grandfather first, then the city's Hollows. |
| **Fear** | Being needed. The city says Halvar was a coward and Maren saved them. Edda believes that whoever the dam needs, it takes. |
| **Debt** | The city has fed Brita and Halvar for a century, as charity for "the builder's shame". Edda pays the household's Tithe in her own memories, since a Hollow can't pay. She has less to offer than she seems to. |

### The recruitment story

Each side quest reveals part of Edda. Her trust follows Signe's deeds, like the hermit's in chapter 1.

- **Ask.** Once she trusts Signe, Edda offers the one memory of Halvar seeming to look at her as a child: "If you are going to stand where she stood, take something that remembers him." Signe gains Kin. Edda keeps feeding him, but no longer knows why it felt like more than duty.
- **Bargain.** With some trust and a kept chapter 1 promise, Edda offers on a condition: if Signe ever can, she gives Halvar back. The promise is saved for chapter 5 and ties to the "give the memories back" ending.
- **Take.** Anyone can be hollowed (**Design**). The obvious place is the Hollow-house, in front of her helpers, mirroring Maren at the dam, and it will be seen. Kin at once, +10 Power and Edda's whole life. Brita then feeds two Hollows.
- **Fail.** If the dam breaks first, Edda leaves with the Hollows and can still be met in the refugee camps. Trust is harder, but the Keystone is not lost.

## The crisis: the dam

**Design:** ask the builder (only if trust is earned), hold the dam with your own Keepsakes, call on your Hearth, hollow him, or evacuate and lose the city.

**Draft:** partway through the chapter a crack opens in the crest and the flood-bell rings. The city then has **three rests**. The design's builder is **Gudmund**, today's dam-master (Craft), who lost the keeping to the Tithe.

| Route | What Signe does | What it costs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Asking** | With Kin from Edda, she crafts the first cross-domain Weave by the fire: Mend + area + Kin, *stone that knits like bone*. Järnfors Hearth members stand on the crest with her and add Power for the casting. Anchored, the dam holds for good. | Edda's memory, plus an anchor: an Offering (1 max Power for good), a Keepsake, or an Echo that then lives in the dam. |
| **Taking** | Hollows Gudmund on the crest as Maren took Halvar: +10 Power and his whole keeping, enough to hold the crack until it is mended. Or hollows Edda for the Weave above. | A Hollow, an Echo, the hunger. The masons see it; the city knows. |
| **Preparing** | Opens Halvar's sealed relief cut over the three rests: Weaken the plug, Strengthen the lower walls, Push the sluice carts clear. What she carries in her three Weave slots decides how much each trip gets done. | The cut floods the lower quarter. Nobody needs to die, but homes and forges are lost, and the poor pay. |
| **Spending herself** | Burns Keepsakes to hold the crack with raw Craft. About two are enough. | Whatever she burns. |

**Failing is allowed (Design).** **Draft:** if the clock runs out, the city is evacuated in the night and the dam breaks at dawn. The gorge becomes rooftops in brown water, refugee camps line the north road, unfinished side quests continue there, and the statue is gone. Nobody blames Signe. People simply say "when the dam went".

Every route can be found before the bell rings. A player with a Hearth here has helpers and a plan; one who skipped the side quests stands alone on the crest, where hollowing looks like the only way (**Design** intent).

## Side quests

**Draft throughout.** Each reveals part of Edda, changes the city visibly, carries biased lore and shows the hollowing shortcut.

### 1. Brita's bowl

Brita spoons broth into a man who looks past her, twice a day. She asks one thing: was he a coward?

- **Investigate:** the statue's version, the apprentices' hall (quest 2), and Halvar's broken drawing table, which Signe can Mend to find what he carved underneath.
- **Endings:** the truth, a kind lie, or nothing. With the truth, Brita has herself wheeled to the dam with Halvar, and his name is scratched beside Maren's on the statue.
- **Offering:** her memory of the morning he refused ("I have carried it long enough"). Her hands keep feeding him after she forgets why.
- **Shortcut:** hollow Brita. Two Hollows at the window; Edda never offers.
- **Reveals:** who Edda is.

### 2. The keeping

Gudmund's masons patch cracks they no longer understand; the gallery inside the dam has collapsed.

- **Solutions:** Mend the gallery (Mend II) and read the stone herself; find the apprentices' tally-sticks, which record every drain; or hollow Gudmund for the keeping whole.
- **World change:** lanterns in the gallery, masons going in at dawn.
- **Offering:** his first stone, once he admits he has forgotten the rest (necessity, like Ebbe).
- **Reveals:** the sealed relief cut, and Edda's fear: the city has needed someone every generation since Halvar.

### 3. The Tithe-house

People bound for Kronvall wait here. A sick boy, Arn, was left behind when the last sled went north.

- **Lilja:** Arn shared the room with a girl from Kallsjö who "talked about swimming". Recognition depends on *the swim* and *Why you left*.
- **Solutions:** Mend a crate into a bed and carry him to the Hollow-house; talk the clerk into striking his name; or hollow the clerk, who knows the Tithe's road to Kronvall.
- **Offering:** the clerk's memory of the first child he signed away (guilt).
- **Reveals:** Edda's debt: a page of the ledger, her household Tithe paid in her own memories for twenty years.

### 4. The cramp-thieves

Families from the lower quarter pry iron cramps out of the dam at night to sell; they know it will fail. Stopping them slows the crack. Letting them go costs one rest from the clock, but those families are gone before the flood. This holds the fight below.

## Maren's mark

**Draft throughout.**

- **The statue:** Saint Maren on the crest, one hand flat on the stone. "She held the water." Scratched below, long ago: "with whose hands?"
- **The apprentices' hall:** nine marks on the lintel, the ninth scratched out. With Gerda's gift or the island doorpost, Signe reads it: **Jon**. Maren's brother followed her here and was apprenticed to Halvar. She hollowed his master in front of him.
- **The vigil:** the apprentices' descendants stand on the crest one silent night a year. The city thinks they honour Maren. They are watching for the failure Halvar predicted.

**Keepsake mirror:** *the swim*, Maren's brother. Jon, the brother she kept alive in Kallsjö, watched her take a man; this seeds the soldier who later opposes her. A quieter echo of *the coat*: Brita's century of feeding is the purest free gift in the game, given to someone who can never receive it.

## The fight: cramp-thieves on the crest

**Draft,** on the wolf fight's rules (**Design**/decisions.md): two actions a turn, enemies show their move before acting, three wounds and Signe goes back to her last rest, Push moves only made things.

- **Where:** the crest at night: plank walkways over the spillway, cramp crates, a windlass cart.
- **Who:** three thieves led by **Knut**, a forge-hand, with pry-bars and a lantern.
- **How:** Weaken a plank and a thief drops to the ledge below, hurt and out of the fight. Push a crate or the cart into one; two hits and he yields. Two down and the last runs.
- **Avoid it:** tell Knut what quest 2 revealed, or promise his family a place on the relief route (a saved promise); Strengthen the cramps in daylight so there's nothing to take; or get Gudmund to post masons.
- **After:** spare, ask for an Offering, or hollow (**Design**). Hollowing Knut gives +10 Power on the spot, and the masons on the towers can see.
- **Restrained:** falls land on the ledge; nobody drowns, no gore.
